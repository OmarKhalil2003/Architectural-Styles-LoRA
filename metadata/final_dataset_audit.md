## 2. Key Architectural Vocabulary per Style

- **Brutalism Architecture**: Board-marked concrete (*béton brut*), heavy cantilevered masses, deep-set window embrasures, structural piers, waffle slab soffits, brise-soleil, bush-hammered aggregate texture.
- **Bauhaus Architecture**: Planar smooth white stucco, industrial steel-sash ribbon windows, continuous glass curtain walls, flat roof terraces, unornamented cubic volumes, cantilevered balconies with tubular railings.
- **International Style**: Modular steel-and-glass curtain wall grids, slender cylindrical pilotis, open ground-floor glass boxes, horizontal ribbon glazing, planar rectilinear geometry, flat roof slabs.
- **Postmodern Architecture**: Playful broken pediments, oversized classical columns, polychrome/pastel facade panels, decorative keystones, curved architraves, eclectic historical quotations.
